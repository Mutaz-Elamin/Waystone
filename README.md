## About 

Waystone is a survival rouge lite game focused on getting a knight safely back home. Cursed upon a dangerous journey you must traverse vastly distant lands slowly progressing using portals to find yourself safely at home. Build yourself up by gathering resources to combat the fierce ogre in each location that will give you what you need to activate the portal for your next location. Don’t take too long though as mother nature herself fights against you with the world itself draining more of your life essence away each continuous day in a place. 

**Genre:** survival, rogue lite, first person, fantasy, RPG 

**Status:**  Complete

## Controls 

**WASD:** Forwards/backwards, strafe left/right 

**Spacebar:** Jump 

**L Shift:** Sprint 

**L Ctrl:** Crouch, Slide 

**WASD (double tap):** Dash 

**Left Click:** Attack, Move Item, Craft 

**Right Click:** Drop Item 

**E:** Interact, Pickup Item, Use Portal, Summon Altar Boss 

**Tab:** Open/Close Inventory
